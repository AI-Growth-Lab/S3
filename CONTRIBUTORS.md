# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who helps build the S3 project! This includes people who **propose analyses**
that get adopted, as well as those who contribute code, documentation, or review.

## How you get listed here

When your proposed analysis is validated and added to the project (see
[`docs/proposal-process.md`](docs/proposal-process.md)), or when you contribute code/docs, you're
added to the table below and to the repository's contributor history — either by opening the pull
request yourself, or via a `Co-authored-by:` commit trailer naming you.
